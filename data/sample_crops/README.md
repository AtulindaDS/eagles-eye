# 🌿 Sample Crop Imagery for Testing & Model Evaluation

This directory holds test crop photos for evaluating the Gemini multimodal crop diagnostic model.

## Recommended Test Folders & Categories
- `banana/`: Healthy banana leaves and leaves exhibiting Banana Bacterial Wilt (BXW)
- `cassava/`: Healthy cassava leaves and leaves showing Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD) or Cassava Brown Streak Disease (CBSD)
- `coffee/`: Healthy coffee foliage and leaves showing Coffee Leaf Rust (CLR) or Coffee Berry Disease (CBD)
- `maize/`: Healthy maize leaves and Fall Armyworm (FAW) damage
- `inconclusive/`: Blurry, underexposed, or non-crop test images for edge-case validation

> **Note:** Do not commit very large high-resolution datasets (>100MB). Keep 10–20 curated lightweight test images for hackathon benchmarking.
